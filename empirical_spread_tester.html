Formula: P(margin = k | proj) ∝ φ((k - proj) / σ) × pv(|k|)

The normal bell curve (σ=13) is reweighted by the empirical point values at each integer margin. This ensures:
• Valid probabilities (always 0-100%)
• Distribution centered on the model projection
• Near margin 0: pv(0)≈0 (no ties), pv(1)=2.2% → distribution has a dip, reducing cover probability when cushion crosses there
• Near margin 4-8: pv peaks at ~3.95% → distribution is fatter here than the normal predicts
• Same cushion at different positions → different cover probabilities

pv(x) = smoothed Unabated NBA point values. The chart shows the reweighted distribution (colored bars) vs the pure normal (faint white line).
